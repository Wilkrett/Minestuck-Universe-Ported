Format: [Aspect] [Classpect] [Offensive/Defensive/Utility] Name - Description
"Any Class"/"Any Aspect" means no requirement on that half; a name in parentheses is a cosmetic
flavor tag only (shown for fictional flavor, does not gate equipping).
